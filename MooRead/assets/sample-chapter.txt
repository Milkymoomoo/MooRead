MooRead can open images, scanned pages, EPUBs, PDFs, and plain text.

When you press Play, the local voice engine speaks the current block while
the next block is already being prepared in a warm buffer. After a block is
finished, its audio is discarded so memory stays flat even in a long book.

Voice profiles are JSON files. Import one to change rate, pitch, diction,
lexicon, and (on Windows) a Piper neural model path.
